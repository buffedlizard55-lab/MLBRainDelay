🌧 Delay Feed

Unofficial fan project. Game data via the public MLB StatsAPI (statsapi.mlb.com) — the same feeds that power MLB.com Gameday — including MLB's own per-game weather string, roof type, official delay minutes and postponement / suspension status. Forecasts and alerts come from the U.S. National Weather Service (api.weather.gov) at each ballpark's coordinates, or Environment and Climate Change Canada (api.weather.gc.ca) where the NWS has no coverage. Forecast-risk chips are a transparent rule over the official forecast, not a prediction — the rules and every source URL are on the verification page. Anything the sources disagree on is flagged 🚩 for manual review, never corrected or hidden. Not affiliated with MLB, MLB Advanced Media, NOAA or ECCC.
