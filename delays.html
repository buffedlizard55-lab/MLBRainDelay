Unofficial fan project. Delays are read from the official game status on the MLB StatsAPI schedule (status.detailedState / statusCode / reason, vocabulary from GET /api/v1/gameStatus), the official status-change advisories in the play-by-play (playEvents[].details.eventType = "game_advisory", description "Status Change - Delayed: Rain" with startTime/endTime), MLB's own delay figure (gameInfo.delayDurationMinutes) and the printed box-score time line (T: 2:50 (3:50 delay)). Postponements / suspensions use the same status fields plus rescheduleDate / rescheduledFrom / resumeDate. Forecasts and alerts come from the National Weather Service (api.weather.gov points → hourly forecast → active alerts at the venue's own coordinates) or Environment and Climate Change Canada (api.weather.gc.ca city-page weather) for Toronto; venues neither covers fall back to Open-Meteo model data and are flagged. The forecast-risk bucket is a fixed rule over those numbers (documented on the verification page), not a prediction. Rows marked observed are status flips this browser saw between polls and carry this browser's clock, never an official time. When the official figures disagree with each other the row is flagged 🚩 and nothing is corrected or hidden. Not affiliated with MLB, MLB Advanced Media, NOAA or ECCC.
